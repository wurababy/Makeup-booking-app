For the best results

  • Face the camera directly.
  • Use natural or bright lighting.

Your privacy

Why I ask for photos: so I can notice your features, such as face shape and eye shape, and suggest makeup that suits you.

How they’re used: your photos are analysed once to create your suggestions. They are never shared, sold or used for anything else.

Storage: by default, your photos are deleted as soon as your results are ready.

You can ask for your photos to be deleted at any time by emailing hello@yourstudio.com.
